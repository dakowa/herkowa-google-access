Privacy policy

This policy describes Herkowa, a personal-use application operated by Damian Kowalski. Contact: dakowa@gmail.com.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner’s Google consent, Herkowa reads email headers and short previews to prioritise personal mail. Its mail reader does not download full message bodies or attachments. It reads selected calendar information to prepare daily and weekly summaries. It creates Gmail drafts and adds events to the owner’s primary calendar at the owner’s request.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in a local file restricted to the operating-system user. Mail records, decisions, reminders, and related working data are kept locally. Selected summaries, including flagged senders and subjects and calendar titles, can be copied to the owner’s Google Drive folder through Drive for desktop. Private briefings and alerts can be delivered to the owner through Telegram. These files and messages remain subject to the owner’s account and folder settings.

External processing

For mail triage, Herkowa can send a redacted sender name and domain, subject, and up to approximately 300 characters of preview to Typesafe/Jev at api.typesafe.ai. The redaction code removes email addresses, links, IBAN patterns, and certain number patterns. Redaction is not guaranteed to remove all personal information. Employer-domain mail configured for exclusion is not sent for scoring.

Google provides the authorized APIs and optional Drive synchronization. Telegram provides private briefing delivery. Typesafe/Jev provides triage inference. Provider handling and retention are governed by their applicable terms and privacy policies. Herkowa does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or contain a model-training function.

Retention and control

Local records and copied summaries remain until the owner deletes them or changes the configuration. No automatic universal retention limit is promised. Revoking access stops future API access but does not delete previously stored local records, Drive files, or Telegram messages. The owner can remove those separately.

The owner can revoke authorization at Google Account connections. To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by this application, contact the operator above.
