PERSONAL ASSISTANT

Herkowa

Herkowa is Damian Kowalski’s private assistant. It runs on his own computer and helps him review personal mail, prepare drafts, and organise his calendar.

Google access

  • Reads mail headers and short previews for inbox triage.
  • Creates Gmail drafts when requested. Herkowa does not send mail.
  • Reads selected calendars and adds events to the owner’s primary calendar when requested. It does not invite guests.

Google authorization currently covers reading emails, composing new drafts, reading calendars, and viewing/editing calendar events. Application code restricts use further.

Private tool, public information

This website explains the application. It does not provide a login, request Google access, or expose the assistant, mailbox, calendar, or credentials. The app is for its owner’s personal use and is not offered as a public service.

See the privacy policy for data use and the terms for access limits.
